This plot aggregates SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values across all samples and shows how much each feature contributes on average to the model’s predictions, separately for each class. Class 0 refers to Down, Class 1 for Flat, and Class 2 for Up. Higher bars mean greater overall influence on the model’s output.
Key Takeaways
1. Volume Is the Most Influential Feature
•	volume has the highest mean SHAP value across all classes.
•	Its impact is particularly strong for Class 1 (Flat) and Class 0 (Down), indicating: 
o	Trading activity plays a dominant role in class separation.
o	The model likely relies on liquidity or participation intensity to infer market movement/regime.
✅ This aligns well with financial intuition: volume often confirms price action and momentum.
2. Price-Based Features Dominate the Top Rankings
Among the next most important features:
•	close
•	high
•	low
•	open
These features:
•	Contribute meaningfully across all three classes
•	Suggest the model heavily uses raw OHLC price structure
📌 Interpretation: The model is learning price-level and intraday range patterns, not just derived indicators.
3. Technical Indicators Add Strong Complementary Signals
Notable technical indicators:
•	rsi
•	macd_histogram
•	macd
•	signal_line
•	ema_12, ema_26, sma_20
Observations:

📈 This indicates:
•	Momentum and overbought/oversold dynamics are critical to the model’s decision-making.
•	Trend smoothers help, but are secondary.
4. Sentiment Features Are Meaningful
Sentiment-related features:

🧠 Interpretation:

•	This is typical in financial prediction: sentiment enhances but rarely replaces price/volume data.
